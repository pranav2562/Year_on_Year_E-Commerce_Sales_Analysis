# Year on Year E-Commerce Sales Analysis <br>

## Objective: Why are the sales declining? <br>

## Source: Data set is imported from Kaggle. <br>
It was a messy data: <br>
•	It had duplicate rows <br>
•	Inconsistent Countries, Payment modes <br>
•	Dates were in different format etc. <br>

## Data Processing: I cleaned the data set using Python <br>

## Data Visualization: <br>
•	With that data set and the help of Power BI, I prepared the charts showing Revenue, Quantity & Product category by country. Which explains the larger picture, also added products under drill with down function to show the actual picture of the business. <br>
•	To focus on sales by product and which country or city, created graphs and which compare orders received, revenue & quantities sold. <br>
•	With the discounts, I compared different category & their products. Aim was to understand which category or product sold over discounts and especially at what level of discount. <br>
•	With the Order status, I compared different category & their products. Aim was to see overall proportionate cancelled and returned products or product category [Limitation: reason was not mentioned]. I filtered out [Shipping & Pending] as they are under process and their status will change to [Delivered, Returned & Cancelled]. <br>

## Outcome (according to the data set): <br>
•	Sales are dropped and the order quantities were also dropped, so dropping sales could be a reason. <br>
•	Providing discounts is not a problem because about 35% of business sales are from zero discounts. <br>
•	Ratings could not be the reason, because it hasn’t changed over time. <br>
